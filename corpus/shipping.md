# Shipping and Delivery

## Service levels

| Service   | Carrier code | Transit time  | Tracking | Cutoff (local) |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Standard  | STD-GRD      | 5-7 days      | Yes      | 15:00          |
| Expedited | EXP-2DA      | 2 days        | Yes      | 17:00          |
| Overnight | OVN-1DA      | Next business | Yes      | 18:30          |
| Freight   | FRT-LTL      | 7-14 days     | Partial  | 12:00          |

Orders placed after the cutoff are processed the following business day. Cutoffs are
in the warehouse's local time, not the customer's.

## Where we ship

We ship to all fifty US states, Canada, the UK, and the EU. We do not ship to PO boxes
for anything above five kilograms, because our contracted carriers will not accept
oversized parcels for box delivery.

## Delays outside our control

Weather, customs inspection, and carrier capacity during peak season can add time
beyond the transit estimates above. The estimates describe typical transit once the
parcel is moving; they are not guarantees, and we do not refund shipping charges for
delays attributable to the carrier.

## Damaged or lost parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the packaging before opening it. Claims filed
without photographs of the exterior are routinely denied by the carrier, and we cannot
override that. Report the damage within 48 hours of delivery.

A parcel is considered lost after fifteen business days with no tracking movement. At
that point we ship a replacement at no cost and file the claim ourselves.

## Freight deliveries

Freight shipments are curbside only. The driver is not required to bring the pallet
inside or to a specific floor. Arrange for someone able to receive and move the pallet,
and note that liftgate service must be requested at the time of order — it cannot be
added once the shipment is in transit.
