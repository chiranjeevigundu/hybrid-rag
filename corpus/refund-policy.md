# Refund Policy

This policy is issued as document **POL-4471** and supersedes POL-3980.

## Timeframes

The window begins on the delivery date recorded by the carrier, not the order date.

| Region        | Window  | Restocking fee | Return shipping |
|---------------|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| Domestic      | 30 days | $0.00          | Prepaid label   |
| International | 60 days | $12.50         | Customer pays   |
| Wholesale     | 90 days | $0.00          | Customer pays   |
| Clearance     | 14 days | $25.00         | Customer pays   |

International orders get a longer window because customs clearance regularly adds two
to three weeks before the customer ever sees the parcel.

## Condition requirements

Items must be unused and in original packaging. We do not require the outer shipping
box, only the retail packaging. Opened software, perishables, and custom-cut materials
are final sale and cannot be returned under any circumstance.

## How to start a return

Email returns@example.com with the order number. You will receive an RMA number within
one business day. Parcels arriving without an RMA number written on the outside are
refused at the dock and returned to sender at the customer's expense.

## Refund method and timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds typically appear in three
to five business days once we process them, though the exact timing is set by the
issuing bank and is outside our control. Bank transfers take seven to ten business
days. Store credit is issued immediately.

## Exceptions

Orders placed under promotional code SPRING24 are subject to a 30-day window
regardless of region. Items damaged in transit are replaced rather than refunded and
fall under the shipping claims process, not this policy.
